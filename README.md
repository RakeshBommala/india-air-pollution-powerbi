# 🌍 India Air Pollution – Environmental Risk Intelligence Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents a **data-driven analysis of air pollution across India**, transforming large-scale air quality monitoring records into actionable environmental insights.

Using **Power BI**, the project develops a series of interconnected dashboards that move from **situational awareness to evidence-based intervention planning**.

The overall decision-making framework follows:

> **Detect → Assess → Explain → Track → Secure → Act**

The project focuses on identifying pollution hotspots, understanding geographic and seasonal patterns, tracking historical pollution risk, evaluating data confidence, and recommending realistic intervention strategies.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

The major objectives of the project are:

* 🌐 Establish the **national air pollution picture**
* 📍 Identify **geographic pollution hotspots**
* 🏭 Explain pollution patterns across **industrial and urban areas**
* 🌦️ Analyze the influence of **seasonal variations**
* 📈 Track **historical pollution risk trajectories**
* 🔎 Assess **data completeness, evidence strength, and uncertainty**
* 🛡️ Improve environmental **risk and decision security**
* 🎯 Recommend **realistic and location-specific interventions**

## 📊 Evidence Strength

Data reliability is considered as part of the decision-making process.

| Pollutant | Evidence Strength | Classification |
| --------- | ----------------: | -------------- |
| **NO₂**   |            99.98% | 🟢 Strong      |
| **SO₂**   |            99.98% | 🟢 Strong      |
| **RSPM**  |            98.58% | 🟢 Strong      |
| **SPM**   |            45.83% | 🟠 Limited     |

> **Note:** Evidence strength is used to communicate confidence in the available monitoring records and to avoid treating uncertain measurements as equally reliable evidence.

## 🚀 Action Framework

The analytical findings are converted into an actionable framework:

### 1. 🔴 Prioritize

Focus resources on **high-pollution and high-risk hotspots**.

### 2. 🔎 Validate

Cross-check identified priorities with relevant:

* CPCB frameworks
* CAQM action plans
* State-level pollution control plans
* Local environmental assessments

### 3. 🎯 Target

Select interventions according to the **local pollution context**, rather than applying the same solution everywhere.

### 4. 📡 Monitor

Continuously reassess pollution levels and monitor the effectiveness of implemented interventions.

### 5. 📈 Evaluate

Compare pollution indicators **before and after intervention** to determine whether risk has been reduced.

---

## 💡 Key Project Idea

The main goal is not simply to visualize pollution data.

Instead, the project aims to answer:

> **Where is pollution highest, why might it be occurring, how confident are we in the evidence, and where should intervention and monitoring resources be prioritized?**

This transforms a traditional **data visualization dashboard** into an **Environmental Risk Intelligence and Decision Support System**.
